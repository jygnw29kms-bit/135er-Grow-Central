# DF100M Integration

Current target: Mars Hydro DF100M / MZ_MZF002.

BLE discovery, connect/disconnect and GATT inspection are implemented. Notification capture and the speed protocol remain experimental.

Candidate UUIDs and strings are reverse-engineering observations, not official vendor protocol documentation. Keep `DF100M_ALLOW_WRITES=false` until the protocol is observed, replayed and validated.
